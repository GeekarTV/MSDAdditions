# MSD Additions

This is an open-source sidemod for Mega Showdown.

# Content

The goal of this mod is to rework how to obtain gimmicks such as (and as of right now only) Mega Evolution, as without a minimap, Megastones/Keystones can be a pain to find without much reliable way to locate them.

Later on, you will also be able to keep or disable Megaroids and Megasites's spawning through config (and also chance Mega Clusters's growth chance.)

I am aware the quality of this mod is kinda poor, and that's why feedback would b really helpful !
As of right now, I consider the mod being in an alpha/beta state, as a lot of things are missing or work in progress (such as worldgen and textures), hope you will still enjoy trying out the mod !

I plan to also add other functionalities to this mod later on, but right now I'm going to focus on my studies, so it'll stay in a poor state for a good long while...
